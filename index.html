About Our Apps

Bear Boutigue creates cozy, creative, and easy-to-use apps for everyday life, creativity, music, memories, planning, and children.

Our apps are designed with a recognizable Bear Boutigue style — soft colors, bears, illustrations, animations, and a warm atmosphere.

My Cutie Diary

My Cutie Diary is a personal diary created for preserving memories, thoughts, moods, family stories, and the little moments of everyday life.

Users can create memories with text, photos, videos, audio, and voice recordings, organize them by date, search through previously saved memories, and return to important moments from months or years ago.

The app includes a Memory Calendar, Memory Search, Mood of the Day, monthly Mood Statistics, relaxing built-in audio and video, background audio playback, a full-featured audio player, daily kind reminders, and convenient sharing of memories and attachments.

Memories can also be added to dates from the distant past, making the app suitable for preserving family history, stories about grandparents, ancestors, and other meaningful memories.

My Cutie Diary is designed to grow together with the user and become a personal collection of memories for many years.

Bear's Notes

Bear's Notes is a creative note-taking app for writing, ideas, books, plans, personal notes, and special moments.

Users can create and edit text, choose between Marker Felt and Times New Roman fonts, change text alignment, undo and redo changes, and add photos, videos, audio, files, voice recordings, and scanned documents.

Saved notes are organized in a calendar, making it easy to return to a specific date. Notes can also be found using search.

The app includes audio playback with its own player, background playback, sharing of complete notes as ZIP files or individual attachments, handwritten signatures and illustrations saved in PNG format, and iCloud synchronization between supported devices.

Bear's Notes is available across iPhone, iPad, and Mac, allowing notes and creative ideas to stay accessible on different Apple devices.

Baby Bear Art

Baby Bear Art is a simple and playful drawing app created especially for young children.

Children can choose colors, adjust line thickness, use an eraser, undo or redo actions, clear the canvas, and create their own drawings in an easy-to-understand environment.

The app includes two cute animated screens that create a fun and magical atmosphere.

Finished drawings can be saved as PNG images with a transparent background.

These drawings can become special family memories and can also be used for personal creative ideas, such as printed keepsakes, postcards, family souvenirs, or prints on clothing.

Baby Bear Art is designed for use at home, while traveling, or whenever a child wants to draw and create.

Baby Bear Art is free to use and does not require a subscription. It does not contain advertising.

Bear Alarm & Calendar

Bear Alarm & Calendar is a cozy everyday planning app that combines alarms, calendar tools, reminders, lists, and personal planning features in one Bear Boutigue environment.

Users can create alarms with labels, repeat days, and selected sounds, organize events in the calendar, and receive reminders before planned events.

The app also includes planning and tracking tools, saved lists, a travel checklist, weight tracking, notes, and day-by-day task marking.

The calendar supports day, week, month, and year views.

Bear Alarm & Calendar automatically follows the device’s regional time format and supports both 12-hour and 24-hour time systems.

It is designed as a warm and convenient space for organizing everyday plans at home, at work, or while traveling.

Music Bears

Music Bears is a cozy music player created around animated bears and a soft, magical atmosphere.

Users can import their own music files, organize tracks into folders, and enjoy music together with animated bear visuals.

The app is designed for relaxing listening, personal music collections, and creating a warm visual experience around favorite tracks.

Music playback can continue in the background, allowing users to continue listening while using other parts of the device.

Music Bears combines music, animations, and the recognizable Bear Boutigue visual style in one simple player.

Bear Power Music

Bear Power Music is a powerful music player with expressive bear visuals and dynamic animations.

Users can import downloaded music and their own original audio files, organize tracks into folders, and play music directly inside the app.

The app is suitable for everyday listeners as well as DJs, producers, musicians, and artists who want to enjoy their own tracks in a unique animated environment.

Bear Power Music also supports Apple Music for eligible users with an active Apple Music subscription. Users can search for songs, artists, and albums, create Apple Music folders, organize tracks, and listen to supported radio content directly inside the app.

Music can continue playing when the device is locked or while other apps are being used.

Folders and individual audio files can also be shared through available system sharing options, such as AirDrop, Telegram, and other supported services.

Bear Power Music is available on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Magic Bear Tales

Magic Bear Tales is a magical digital book containing a collection of 20 fairy tales for children.

The stories are created around teddy bears and are accompanied by colorful illustrations and gentle animations that help bring each fairy tale to life.

Children can turn pages like in a real book, explore illustrations, watch animations, and enjoy stories together with their parents.

Magic Bear Tales is designed for reading at home, before bedtime, or while traveling.

The app supports multiple languages and provides a warm, colorful, and child-friendly reading experience in the Bear Boutigue style.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

All Bear Boutigue apps are designed with respect for user privacy.

Bear Boutigue does not collect, sell, or use users’ personal information for advertising or tracking.

Our apps do not create advertising profiles and do not sell user information to third parties.

Information We Collect

Bear Boutigue apps do not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Precise location
  • Contact lists
  • Browsing history
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Personal information for advertising or tracking purposes

User Content and iCloud

Content created inside supported Bear Boutigue apps may be stored locally on the user’s device or synchronized through Apple iCloud when cloud synchronization is available.

This may include content such as notes, memories, attachments, calendar information, or other information created by the user inside the app.

Bear Boutigue does not maintain its own database containing users’ personal content.

When Apple iCloud is used, synchronization and cloud storage are provided through Apple services and are subject to Apple’s privacy and security protections.

Advertising

Bear Boutigue paid apps do not contain third-party advertising.

Access to premium apps or premium features may be provided through subscriptions processed and managed by Apple through the user’s App Store account.

Baby Bear Art

Baby Bear Art is free to use.

Baby Bear Art does not contain advertising and does not require a subscription.

Bear Boutigue does not collect personal information through Baby Bear Art.

Device Permissions

Bear Boutigue apps use only the device features required for their available functionality.

An app does not access a protected device feature unless that feature is required by the app and the operating system provides the appropriate permission.

Features and available permissions may differ between iPhone, iPad, and Mac versions.

Third Parties

Bear Boutigue does not sell users’ personal information to third parties.

Apple services may be used for features such as iCloud synchronization, App Store subscriptions, Apple Music, and other Apple-provided functionality available inside individual apps.

Data Security

Because Bear Boutigue does not maintain its own database of users’ personal app content, content remains on supported user devices or within Apple services when Apple cloud functionality is used.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app functionality changes or when new Bear Boutigue apps are released.

The current version of this Privacy Policy will remain available on the official Bear Boutigue website.
